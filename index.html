Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

AI Mood Detector collects the following types of information:

  • Mood data from various detection methods (camera, voice, text, questionnaire)
  • User preferences and app settings
  • Anonymous usage statistics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide personalized mood tracking and insights
  • Improve our AI mood detection algorithms
  • Enhance user experience

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of stored data
  • Secure cloud backup
  • Limited data access

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal mood data with third parties. Any aggregated, anonymized data may be used for research purposes.

5. User Controls

You have full control over your data, including:

  • Opting out of cloud sync
  • Deleting mood history
  • Disabling specific detection methods

6. Compliance

AI Mood Detector complies with data protection regulations and prioritizes user privacy and consent.
